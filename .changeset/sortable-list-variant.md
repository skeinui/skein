Sortable: a `variant` prop. `tile` (the default, as before) is a card per item; `list` is plain rows with no fill, border or gap, lit under the pointer or keyboard focus, with a faint drag handle: the look for a short list inside a popover or menu, such as which columns of a table to show and in which order. Every theme style draws it (`data-variant="list"`, now part of the theme coverage check).
